
# Generative AI for Middle School: A 6-Week Journey

**Course Philosophy:** Hands-on first, concepts follow curiosity. We will build something real and have fun doing it.

**Instructor:** Your Name Here

**Students:** 3

**Schedule:** Once a week, 1 hour, for 6 weeks

---

## Week 1: The Magical Mind of the Machine

*   **Big Question:** How do computers "think"?
*   **Activity:** We'll play with [Teachable Machine](https://teachablemachine.withgoogle.com/) to train a computer to recognize our faces, our drawings, or our poses. We'll see how quickly we can teach it, and also how easily we can trick it!
*   **What We'll Learn:** We'll get a feel for what "training" an AI means. We'll learn about inputs and outputs, and how AI uses them. We'll also have a simple, non-technical introduction to how computers work.

## Week 2: The AI Poet and Storyteller

*   **Big Question:** How can a computer understand and write stories?
*   **Activity:** We'll become "prompt engineers." We'll use a fun and safe text-generation tool to write poems, create song lyrics, and even generate a short story. We'll experiment with different prompts to see how we can get the most creative results.
*   **What We'll Learn:** We'll discover what a "prompt" is and how it's the key to talking to an AI. We'll get a simple understanding of how Large Language Models (LLMs) work (think of it as super-powered predictive text).

## Week 3: The AI Artist's Studio

*   **Big Question:** How can a computer create images from just words?
*   **Activity:** Let's make some art! We'll use an image generation tool like [Midjourney](https://www.midjourney.com/) or a free alternative to create amazing images. We'll describe our wildest ideas in words and watch the AI bring them to life.
*   **What We'll Learn:** We'll learn how AI can connect words to images. We'll see how artists are using these tools, and we'll talk about what makes art, "art."

## Week 4: The AI DJ and Sound Designer

*   **Big Question:** Can AI make music?
*   **Activity:** We'll experiment with AI music generation tools like [Google's MusicFX](https://artsandculture.google.com/experiment/musicfx) to create our own beats, melodies, and sound effects. We'll try to make a theme song for a superhero or the soundtrack for a video game.
*   **What We'll Learn:** We'll explore how AI can understand patterns in music to create new sounds. We'll also get to be creative and make some noise!

## Week 5: Let's Build! Project Brainstorming

*   **Big Question:** What amazing thing can we build with AI?
*   **Activity:** It's time to dream big. As a team, we'll decide on a final project. Will we create an illustrated story with AI images and text? An animation with an AI-generated script? A theme song for our school? We'll brainstorm ideas, choose our favorite, and make a plan.
*   **What We'll Learn:** We'll learn how to work together to come up with creative ideas. We'll think about how to combine the different AI tools we've used to make something new.

## Week 6: Project Showcase and a Look to the Future

*   **Big Question:** What did we make, and what's next for AI?
*   **Activity:** It's showtime! We'll present our final project to each other and celebrate what we've created.
*   **What We'll Learn:** We'll reflect on what we've learned and what we're curious about now. We'll also have a friendly chat about the future of AI, including the cool parts and the things we need to be thoughtful about (like ethics and how AI impacts our lives).
